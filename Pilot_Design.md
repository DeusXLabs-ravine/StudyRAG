# Pilot Design: StudyRAG Deployment for 1,000+ Users

## 1. Primary Objectives and Structure of the Pilot
The primary objective of this pilot is to validate the efficacy of StudyRAG's offline-capable, voice-driven AI teaching platform at scale. We aim to measure improvements in students' comprehension, learning engagement, and retention—particularly in low-bandwidth and resource-constrained environments.

**Structure:**
- **Phase 1: Preparation & Onboarding (Weeks 1-4)**: Partner alignment, finalizing the localized curriculum cache, and initial train-the-trainer workshops for educators.
- **Phase 2: Deployment & Initial Use (Weeks 5-8)**: Rollout of the application to 1,000 users. Daily usage monitoring via cached, auto-syncing analytics.
- **Phase 3: Active Monitoring & Iteration (Weeks 9-16)**: Mid-pilot feedback loops; providing OTA (Over-The-Air) updates when users connect to high-speed nodes.
- **Phase 4: Evaluation & Reporting (Weeks 17-20)**: Final learning assessments, user satisfaction surveys, and stakeholder debriefings.

## 2. Duration of the Pilot
The pilot will run for a total of **5 months (20 weeks)**, aligning with a typical academic semester. This duration ensures we can measure both short-term adoption and long-term habits and educational impacts.

## 3. Participating User Groups
We will deploy the solution to a cohort of **1,200 students** (anticipating a 20% attrition/inactivity rate, ensuring an active base of at least 1,000 users). The groups consist of:
- **Low-Resource & Marginalized Students (60%)**: Students from low-income, rural or peri-urban areas where internet access is intermittent or prohibitively expensive. The offline caching syncs progress seamlessly during brief connectivity windows.
- **Students with Learning Disabilities (20%)**: Specifically targeting students with Dyslexia and ADHD. StudyRAG’s AI voice-tutoring, varied pacing, and screen-reader compliant UI are designed to minimize cognitive overload and reading fatigue.
- **General Cohort (20%)**: A control baseline group from standard-resource environments to provide a comparative metric on engagement and academic performance.

## 4. Potential Partnerships
To successfully reach and support 1,000+ users, the pilot relies on a collaborative ecosystem:

- **Public & System-Level Partnerships (Universities/School Districts)**: Leveraging institutional channels to distribute the software and securely track student progress against curriculum standards.
- **NGOs focusing on Educational Equity**: Partnering with local NGOs to identify, recruit, and support marginalized students who can benefit most from low-bandwidth educational tools.
- **Telecommunication Providers**: Exploring "zero-rating" partnerships to waive data costs when students sync their progress and download new lesson caches.
- **University Accessibility / Disability Services**: To aid in selecting neurodivergent participants and ensuring our feedback rubrics accurately measure accessibility outcomes.

## 5. Responsibilities of Each Partner
- **StudyRAG (Our Team)**: Provide the software, ensure 99.9% uptime, manage data privacy and security, and deliver aggregated analytics reports. Provide technical support and UX iteration.
- **Educational Institutions**: Integrate pilot participation into course crediting/extra credit, encourage student enrollment, and share anonymized academic performance baselines.
- **NGO Partners**: Act as ground-level liaisons. Distribute devices (if necessary), conduct localized onboarding workshops, and gather qualitative, on-the-ground feedback.
- **Accessibility Offices**: Co-design surveys for students with disabilities and help interpret accessibility-related usage patterns.

## 6. Long-Term Outcomes and Persistence Pathways
This pilot is not a temporary intervention; it is designed to establish sustainable educational infrastructure:
- **Ecosystem Integration**: Post-pilot, the AI tutoring models will be deeply integrated into the partner institutions' Learning Management Systems (LMS), moving from a standalone app to an institutional standard.
- **Data-Driven Curriculum Support**: Analytics generated (e.g., where students struggle most, which concepts require more voice-explanations) will provide institutions with actionable insights to permanently improve local curricula.
- **Sustained Offline Access Model**: The low-bandwidth caching system will remain active, establishing an ongoing precedent for accessible software distribution without relying on continuous, high-speed broadband.
