Free, ready-to-use podcast studio — film, shoot, or create your next project with ease!
This compact studio is perfect for podcast filming, indoor action scenes, pre-wedding shoots, wedding stages, parties, and press conferences. It includes a professional green screen for versatile video content and chroma key effects.
Free for personal and commercial use in games, videos, or films — just don’t resell or redistribute it.
All parts are modular, detailed, and easy to edit with non–baked textures. Fully game-ready, requiring no major adjustments — simply set it up and add your own props like tables, microphones, or lights.
The hall features two rooms, with one left empty for your own creative setup.
