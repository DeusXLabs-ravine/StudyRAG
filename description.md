# StudyRAG: Project Description

StudyRAG is an AI-powered educational platform designed to provide students with a personalized, context-aware learning environment. By integrating the latest advancements in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and LLM reasoning (via Google Gemini), StudyRAG bridges the gap between static study materials and active, guided learning.

### Core Value Proposition
Traditional study methods often involve passive reading or generic AI chat that lacks context. StudyRAG allows students to upload their own course documents (PDFs, Docx, Text) and interact with an AI tutor that knows exactly what they are studying. The system adapts its teaching style based on the student's mastery level and proactively identifies areas where the student needs more help.

### Impact & Innovation
- **Personalization**: Unlike generic LLMs, StudyRAG's tutoring is grounded in the student's specific curriculum.
- **Adaptive Pedagogy**: The system evolves with the student, moving from simple analogies for beginners to nuanced technical discussions for advanced learners.
- **Efficiency**: Automating the creation of study modules, flashcards, and quizzes allows students to spend more time learning and less time organizing.
- **Accuracy**: By using RAG, the system minimizes hallucinations and provides direct citations from the student's own documents.

StudyRAG represents the future of AI-assisted education: accurate, adaptive, and deeply personal.
